Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version: we collect nothing.

Drunkoala does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no logins, no analytics, and no internet connection required to play. Everything happens on your device, in the room you're standing in.

Information we collect

None. Drunkoala does not collect any personal information, device identifiers, usage analytics, or player data. Player names entered during a game session exist only in the app's temporary memory while you're playing and are permanently discarded the moment the app closes. Nothing is ever transmitted off your device.

Accounts and sign-in

Drunkoala has no account system. You do not create a profile, sign in, or provide an email address, phone number, or any identifying information to use the app.

Third-party services

Drunkoala v1.0 does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If a future version introduces advertising (for example, via Google AdMob), this policy will be updated in advance, and the change will be reflected in the app's update notes.

Children's privacy

Drunkoala is intended for adults aged 17 and older and includes an age gate at first launch. The app is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age, because the app collects no information from anyone at all.

Data retention

There is nothing to retain. No player data, scores, or game history is saved after a session ends or the app is closed.

Changes to this policy

If Drunkoala's data practices ever change — for example, with the addition of advertising or optional cloud features in a future update — this page will be updated first, and the update will be dated above.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or the app in general can be sent to raghavjm@gmail.com. We respond within 48 hours.
